03 / TARGET CONDITIONS

Diagnostic Classes & Screening Criteria

EggplantGuard recognizes 4 primary crop conditions plus out-of-distribution invalid inputs.

🌿
Foliar Health

Healthy Leaf

Intact green blade lamina with normal venation, clean cuticle, and complete absence of serpentine mines or fungal lesions.

✔ Optimal chlorophyll density ✔ No mechanical or insect wounds
🍂
Pest Infestation

Leaf Miner Damage

Infestation by Liriomyza fly larvae that feed inside the leaf mesophyll, leaving distinct serpentine white/grey tunnels.

✔ Serpentine translucent mines ✔ Diminished photosynthetic area
🍆
Market Grade

Fresh / Market-Ready Fruit

Physiologically mature, firm, glossy fruit exhibiting characteristic cultivar color, vibrant green calyx, and zero punctures.

✔ Firm texture & glossy sheen ✔ Unblemished commercial quality
🐛
Crop Loss Alert

Damaged / Borer-Infested Fruit

Severe economic damage from the Eggplant Fruit & Shoot Borer (Leucinodes orbonalis). Identified by boreholes and frass deposits.

✔ Entrance holes with brown frass ✔ Internal tunneling & soft rot
🛡️

Non-Eggplant Sample Protection:

05 / SYSTEM SPECS

About EggplantGuard

EggplantGuard is an open, transparent agricultural AI diagnostic tool built with Ultralytics YOLO, Flask, and SQLite. Engineered specifically for smallholder growers, agronomists, and IT students, it replaces closed black-box models with an auditable computer vision pipeline.

Python Flask 3 Ultralytics YOLO HSV Color Analysis SQLite 3 HTML5 Canvas Camera

Field Photography Best Practices

  • Focus: Hold the camera 15–30 cm away so leaf veins and fruit skins are in sharp focus.
  • Lighting: Take scans under natural, indirect daylight to prevent glare or deep shadow.
  • Background: Position the leaf or fruit in center view; avoid human hands or busy ground cover.
  • Cleanliness: Wipe dirt and water droplets off the leaf before scanning.
